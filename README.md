# GreenSense
ESP32 Circuit Diagram (DHT22, LDR, Potentiometer (used in place of a Soil Moisture Sensor))

Custom Condition: - Detection of 4 anomalies. Generally, temperature and light, humidity and moisture levels always agree. In the rare cases that they don't, special steps must be taken. (High Temp & Low Light, Low Temp & High Light, High Moisture & Low Humidity, Low Moisture & High Humidity)
